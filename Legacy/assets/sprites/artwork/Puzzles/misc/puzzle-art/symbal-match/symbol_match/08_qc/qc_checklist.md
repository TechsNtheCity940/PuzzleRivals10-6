# QC Checklist

- No words, numbers, logos, or watermarks present.
- All exported assets retain transparent backgrounds.
- Symbol reveals and tile backs use 256x256 frame boxes with 6 frames each.
- `tile_back_shimmer` is the recommended hidden-tile default.
- `tile_back_focus` is the stronger hover/select state.
- Board slots were isolated from the preview board; review slot edges if you want a lighter crop.
