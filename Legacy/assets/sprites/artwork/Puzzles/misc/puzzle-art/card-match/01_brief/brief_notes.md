# D1 Card Flip

## Assumptions
- The uploaded card sheet is the approved visual source for the memory-card mode.
- The numbered face tags and the bottom reference labels are treated as source-guide text and removed from the production outputs.
- The requested runtime animation is a single flip-open strip per card face; flip-close should be played in reverse in Phaser.
- Cards are normalized to 256x256 transparent frames for consistent board placement in a 6x6 grid.
