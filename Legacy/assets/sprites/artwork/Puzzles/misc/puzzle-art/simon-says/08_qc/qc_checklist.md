# QC Checklist

- No words, letters, or numbers visible in any delivered spritesheet
- Transparent background preserved on every exported PNG
- All animation frames stay on a 256x256 grid with 0 spacing
- Diamond silhouettes remain centered with stable scale across frames
- No cropped glows or clipped corners
- No merged frames or stray pixels between slots
- Color rows remain readable and visually distinct
- Phaser animation ranges line up cleanly with row-major frame order

- Square industrial family background removal leaves no checkerboard residue
- Derived pulse strips hold the tile centered without scale drift
