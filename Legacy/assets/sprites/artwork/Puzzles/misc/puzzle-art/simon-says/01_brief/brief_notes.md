# A3 Simon Says Tiles

## Assumptions
- The approved production style is the diamond neon tile family visible in `01-a3_board_preview_v01.png` and `02-a3_floor_tiles_sequence_glow_08f_v01.png`.
- `03-simon_says_tiles_glow_set_v01.png` was treated as supplemental reference only because its square industrial style does not match the active board art family.
- Phaser target uses uniform 256x256 frames, orthographic top-down camera, transparent PNG spritesheets, and looping 8-frame glow animations.
- Tiles are intended for sequence playback, success confirmation, and board readability at small gameplay scale.
