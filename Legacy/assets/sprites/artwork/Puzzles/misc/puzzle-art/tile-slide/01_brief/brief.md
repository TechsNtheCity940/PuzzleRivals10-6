# E1 Sliding Puzzle

## Assumptions
- The separated 3x3 tile set in `tiles.png` is the approved production source.
- The first uploaded board image is used as assembly/reference only.
- Delivered slide strips are destination-anchored: place the sprite on the destination cell and play the direction animation so the tile settles into place.
- The blank tile is included for completeness and uniform naming, even if many implementations only animate the visible piece.
