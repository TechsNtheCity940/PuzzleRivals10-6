# QC Checklist

- No words or labels appear in any delivered tile sprite
- Transparent backgrounds preserved on all PNG exports
- Every strip uses 256x256 frames with zero spacing
- Directional slide strips keep a stable tile scale and consistent frame border
- Final frame settles perfectly centered
- No cropped corners or frame bleed between slots
- Blank tile remains visually distinct and usable as a board hole placeholder

- Solved overlays remain transparent and text-free
