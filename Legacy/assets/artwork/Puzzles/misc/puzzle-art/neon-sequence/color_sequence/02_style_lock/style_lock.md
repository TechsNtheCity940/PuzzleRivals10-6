# Style Lock

Master style prompt:
Clean top-down puzzle UI props, luminous sci-fi buttons and energy orbs, centered composition, consistent rim lighting, readable silhouettes at small scale, premium game-ready polish, transparent background, no text.

Global negative prompt:
words, letters, numerals, logo, watermark, cropped frame, merged frames, scenery, perspective tilt, muddy glow, motion blur.

Sprite-sheet wrapper prompt:
exact horizontal strip, 8 frames, 256x256 per frame, transparent background, stable scale, stable lighting, centered asset, even spacing.

Animation-strip wrapper prompt:
looping pulse or glow behavior, no extra VFX outside frame bounds, no framing labels, no shadows baked to opaque background.
