# QC Checklist

- No words, numbers, or instruction text remain on the delivered cards
- Transparent backgrounds preserved on all PNG exports
- Every strip uses 256x256 frames with zero spacing
- Cards remain centered and uniformly scaled through the flip
- Midpoint edge frames read clearly at small board scale
- No cropped corners or frame bleed between slots
- Flip-close can be achieved by reverse playback without visual mismatch

- Match and mismatch overlays remain transparent and text-free
