# Mirror Maze QC Checklist

- Check for accidental text, symbols, logos, or watermark artifacts.
- Check that all pieces are orthographic top-down with no perspective drift.
- Check that mirror orientation is readable at puzzle scale.
- Check that prisms clearly imply multi-direction beam splitting.
- Check that source and target silhouettes differ immediately.
- Check that beam strips are clean, tileable, and centered.
- Check that activator frames loop smoothly without popping.
- Check that blocked impact frames read as continuous contained energy, not a large detonation.
- Check that blockers visibly stop beams and do not read like mirrors or prisms.
- Check that board tiles support beam readability rather than competing with the FX.
- Check that socket pads remain readable beneath sources and activators.
- Check that the refined mirror v02 sheet has cleaner separation than v01.
- Check that the prism split overlay stays compact enough to layer on top of prism pieces.
- Check that UI icons remain legible at small HUD sizes.
- Check transparency on every exported PNG.
- Check frame spacing so no animation frames bleed into neighbors.
- Check palette consistency across cyan, magenta, amber, lime, and white-hot cores.
